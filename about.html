About the Game

Welcome to the Riddle Game!

Step into a pink, Gorge-ilicious and Gorge-tastic world where you can use the second “B” in the triple B’s—Brain! Solve fun and tricky riddles as you level up from Easy to Extreme.

How to Play

1. Enter your username

2. Choose your level

3. Read the riddle carefully

4. Type your answer and click submit

5. Answer correctly to earn points

Goal

Answer all riddles correctly and become the Ultimate Riddler Master!
